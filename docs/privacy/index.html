Privacy policy

Cycling Tribune

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Cycling Tribune doesn't ask for your name, email or any account, and doesn't track you.

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising. Nothing in the app follows you across apps or websites, and your data isn't sold or shared for advertising.
  • On your device. Your settings, such as whether spoilers are shown, are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app connects to

Cycling Tribune shows public cycling information, so it connects to the internet. Like any internet request, these connections reveal your IP address to the service that answers.

  • Race sites. The app loads pages and images from ProCyclingStats, cyclocross24.com, YouTube and the cycling-news sites listed in the Paddock tab. Links you tap open in an in-app browser, and those sites' own policies apply there.
  • Google Firebase. The cyclocross lists are read from Firebase Firestore, and the list of news sites comes from Firebase Remote Config. Firebase creates a random installation identifier for this. It isn't linked to your identity.
  • Crash reports. Firebase Crashlytics receives a report when the app crashes or hits an unexpected error: device model, iOS version, app version, the state of the app at the time, and the same random installation identifier. It is used only to fix bugs.

What we don't collect

Your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or any health or financial data. Cycling Tribune has no servers of its own that store information about you.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and knowingly collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
